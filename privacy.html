Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Buy an Elephant is provided by Xiru An. This policy describes how the iPhone app handles information.

In short

  • The app does not collect any data. There is no account, no server run by the developer, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details or your Apple Account.

What stays on your iPhone

The app keeps a few small settings in its own storage on your device: which animal is on your home page, how many times you have tapped each animal, the date you first opened the app, and a list of the animals you own so the home page can show them immediately at launch. None of this leaves your device, apart from Apple’s device backup if you use it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Animals are bought through Apple’s In-App Purchase. The app asks Apple which animals your Apple Account owns, and shows the date, price and transaction number from Apple on the adoption certificate. This information comes from Apple and is shown only to you.

Photos

If you choose to save an adoption certificate as an image, iOS asks for permission to add it to your photo library. The app can only add that image. It cannot read your photos.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: dawncx0825@gmail.com.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

买只大象由 Xiru An 提供。本政策说明这款 iPhone App 如何处理信息。

简单说

  • App 不收集任何数据。没有账号、没有开发者自己的服务器、没有广告、没有统计、没有跟踪。
  • 购买完全由 Apple 处理，开发者拿不到你的付款信息和 Apple 账户。

留在你 iPhone 上的东西

App 在自己的存储里保存几项小设置：主页上放的是哪只动物、每只动物被摸了多少下、第一次打开 App 的日期，以及你拥有哪些动物（用来让主页启动时马上显示出来）。这些都不会离开你的设备，除非你使用 Apple 的设备备份。删除 App 即一并删除。

购买

动物通过 Apple 的 App 内购买出售。App 向 Apple 查询你的 Apple 账户拥有哪些动物，并在领养证书上显示 Apple 提供的日期、价格和交易编号。这些信息来自 Apple，只显示给你自己。

相册

如果你选择把领养证书存成图片，iOS 会请求「添加到相册」的权限。App 只能添加这张图，不能读取你的照片。

儿童

本 App 不以儿童为对象，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

变更与联系

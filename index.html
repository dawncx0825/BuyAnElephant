Buy an Elephant

Adopt a cute pet. Tap to play.

Buy an Elephant is a small collection of cute animals for your iPhone. One animal lives on your home page at a time. Tap it and it reacts with its own move and sound. A pigeon comes free; every other animal is a one-time purchase. It requires iOS 17 or later on iPhone.

Privacy Policy 中文

Contact

For help, feedback or privacy questions, email dawncx0825@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version and which animal the question is about.

Buying an animal

Open Shop in the top-right corner, pick an animal and tap the buy button. You can tap the animal on its page first to see how it moves. After the purchase it drops onto your home page and you get its adoption certificate.

Every animal is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There are no ads, bundles, loot boxes or timed offers. What you see is what you get.

New phone, or reinstalled the app

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account. Open Shop, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore Purchases. The app also checks your purchases automatically when it starts.

A purchase is waiting for approval

If Ask to Buy is on for your account, or your bank needs an extra check, the app tells you the animal is waiting. It comes home on its own once the purchase is approved. Nothing needs to be done in the app.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com. A refunded animal leaves your home page.

No sound

Animal sounds follow your iPhone’s silent switch. Turn silent mode off and check the volume. The sounds do not interrupt music you are playing.

买只大象

领养一只小动物，点点它就会动。

买只大象是一个可爱动物收藏 App。主页上一次住一只动物，点它会做自己的动作、发出自己的叫声。鸽子免费送，其他动物都是一次性购买。需要 iOS 17 或以上的 iPhone。

联系

使用问题、建议或隐私问题，请发邮件到 dawncx0825@gmail.com，最好写上 iOS 版本和是哪只动物。

怎么买

点右上角「商店」，选一只动物，点购买按钮。买之前可以在详情页先点点它，看看它怎么动。买到后它会掉到你的主页上，还会给你一张领养证书。

每只都是一次性购买，不是订阅。没有广告、礼包、盲盒或限时活动。

换了手机或重装了 App

购买记录跟着你的 Apple 账户走。打开「商店」，拉到最底下点「恢复购买」。App 启动时也会自动核对。

显示「等待确认」

如果账户开了「询问购买」，或者银行需要额外验证，App 会告诉你动物还在路上。批准之后它会自己到家，不用再操作。

退款

退款由 Apple 处理，请访问 reportaproblem.apple.com。退款后那只动物会离开主页。

没有声音

叫声跟着 iPhone 的静音开关走，关掉静音、调高音量就能听到。叫声不会打断你正在放的音乐。

猫不理我

那就是猫。大概一半的时候它会理你。
